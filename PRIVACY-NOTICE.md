# KeyEcho 0.3.0 privacy notice

KeyEcho is an offline typing-sound utility. It receives global key identity and
press/release events in memory to choose a sound and recognize its mute shortcut.
It does not reconstruct or store typed text, retain a key-event history, transmit
keyboard events, use analytics, display advertising, or require an account.

KeyEcho stores the selected sound profile, volume, and enabled/muted state on the
local computer. User-supplied custom sound profiles also remain local. The app
does not upload these settings or audio files.

The current version contains no telemetry client, cloud service, or automatic
updater. A website or download service used to obtain KeyEcho may operate under
its own privacy policy.

This notice describes the behavior of KeyEcho 0.3.0. It must be reviewed and
updated before release if networking, telemetry, crash reporting, updates,
accounts, or other data-processing features are added.
